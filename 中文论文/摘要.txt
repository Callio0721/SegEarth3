## 摘要

免训练的遥感开放词汇语义分割旨在无需微调的情况下，将任意文本类别精准定位到密集的遥感图像中。最新的基于概念对齐的模型（如 SAM3 和 SegEarth-OV3）虽原生解耦出了存在（Presence）、语义（Semantic）和实例（Instance）等特征读取头，但通常仍依赖单一文本和单一视图进行特征解码。本文发现，这种单一输入无法充分激活模型强大的特征解耦能力。具体而言，这三者承担着截然不同的职责：Presence 需判断类别是否存在，Semantic 负责描述稠密覆盖，而 Instance 则依赖 query 来组织区域与边界。因此，不同的读取头实际上需要不同粒度的信息。为此，我们提出了角色—视图联合专门化（JRV）机制。首先，针对不同读取头的特定需求，JRV 引入了分类别的“角色”文本补充，为各功能头提供精细化的语义引导。其次，由于新增的多元文本信息在单一全局视图下难以被充分利用，尤其是缺乏精确实例分割所需的细腻纹理，JRV 引入了空间对齐的局部（Local）视图。通过将多元文本特征与多尺度视觉特征进行有机结合，JRV 完美贴合了原模型的耦合机制。在8个遥感基准数据集上的实验表明，JRV 的平均 mIoU 从 53.56% 提升至 57.49%，全面超越现有基线并达到了全新的 State-of-the-Art (SOTA) 性能。消融实验证实了角色与视图机制在四分之三的数据集上存在正向交互，自然图像上的泛化实验也进一步证明了该机制的广泛适用性。综上所述，按特征需求细化文本补充，并将其与多尺度视觉视图有机结合，才能更好的释放冻结分割模型的潜能。

## Abstract

Training-free open-vocabulary semantic segmentation in remote sensing aims to localize arbitrary textual categories in dense imagery without model fine-tuning. While recent concept-grounded models (e.g., SAM3 and SegEarth-OV3) inherently decouple features into distinct Presence, Semantic, and Instance readouts, they typically rely on single category descriptions and single visual views for feature decoding. We observe that such single inputs fail to fully activate the models' powerful feature decoupling capabilities. Specifically, these three heads shoulder distinct responsibilities: Presence judges whether a category exists, Semantic describes dense coverage, and Instance relies on queries to organize regions and boundaries. Consequently, different readouts inherently require information of varying granularities. To address this, we propose the Joint Role-View Specialization (JRV) framework. First, to align with the specific requirements of different readouts, JRV introduces categorized "role-specific" text supplements, providing fine-grained semantic guidance tailored to each functional head. Furthermore, we observe that a single global view struggles to fully exploit this enriched textual information, especially lacking the detailed visual textures required for precise instance masks. Therefore, JRV introduces a spatially aligned Local view. By organically integrating these diversified text features with multi-scale visual contexts, JRV perfectly matches the model's native coupling mechanism. Experiments on eight remote-sensing benchmarks demonstrate that JRV improves the average mIoU from 53.56% to 57.49%, outperforming existing baselines and achieving new State-of-the-Art (SOTA) performance. Ablation studies confirm a positive interaction between role and view configurations on 3/4 of the datasets, and evaluations on natural images further prove the broad applicability of JRV. Ultimately, our work demonstrates that purposefully categorizing text supplementation and organically combining it with corresponding visual views is key to unlocking the full potential of frozen segmentation models.
